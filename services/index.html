Engagement

Response tiers.

Every client relationship begins as an assignment. Many become programs. Some become retained partnerships spanning a whole household.

Assignment

A defined engagement: a trip, an event, a sweep or an assessment. Planned in days, delivered to a written scope.

  • Single scope and fixed dates
  • Senior lead from briefing to debrief
  • Operations Desk support throughout

Programmatic

A recurring protective program for a principal or family, combining disciplines under one plan and one lead.

  • Dedicated program lead
  • Consistent officers who know the family
  • Quarterly reviews and testing

Retained

A standing relationship with priority response, retained analysts and pre-planned contingencies for the whole household.

  • Priority deployment worldwide
  • Retained intelligence desk
  • Rehearsed crisis continuity plans
