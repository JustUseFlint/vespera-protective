Overview

Executive protection at Vespera begins long before a principal steps out of a car. Every assignment is built on an advance: routes walked, venues surveyed, hospitals mapped, and every contingency rehearsed, so that the officers at the principal's side can stay calm, close and largely unnoticed.

Capabilities

What the discipline includes.

Close protection officers

Senior officers with backgrounds in government protective details, specialist policing and corporate protection, selected for temperament as much as skill.

72-hour advance protocols

Route reconnaissance, venue walk-throughs, safe-room identification and liaison with local partners, completed and documented before arrival.

Posture on a dial

From a single low-profile officer in business attire to a visible multi-vehicle detail, posture is set by the threat picture and the principal's preferences.

Medical readiness

Every detail carries trauma and cardiac equipment, and at least one officer holds advanced pre-hospital care certification. Nearest trauma centers are pre-mapped.

Protective drivers

Drivers trained in evasive and defensive techniques who also know when the best move is simply a different lane and a quieter street.

Family and residence handover

Seamless coordination with estate teams so protection continues from the office door to the front door without gaps.

Protocol

How an engagement unfolds.

Every step is documented, every handover named, and every operation closes with a written debrief.

  1. T–72h

    Threat and itinerary review

    Intelligence brief, itinerary validation, open-source exposure check and posture recommendation.

  2. T–48h

    Physical advance

    Routes driven at the same hour of travel, venues surveyed, choke points and alternates logged.

  3. T–24h

    Team brief and rehearsal

    Officers, drivers and venue contacts briefed. Emergency actions rehearsed on site.

  4. Arrival

    Live operation

    Detail on station, Operations Desk tracking movement, liaison channels open.

  5. After

    Debrief and record

    Written debrief with observations, anomalies and refinements for the next movement.

Posture

Set to the threat, and to the way you live.

A visible, multi-vehicle detail for elevated threat periods or high-attention events, where deterrence is part of the objective.
