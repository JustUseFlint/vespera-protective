Overview

A residence should feel like a sanctuary, not a compound. Vespera combines resident estate agents with perimeter systems designed alongside your architects and landscape designers, so detection happens at the boundary, decisions happen in the gatehouse, and the household simply lives.

Capabilities

What the discipline includes.

24/7 estate agents

Resident security officers who know the household rhythm, manage access and hand over cleanly with family protection details.

Perimeter optics and sensors

Layered thermal and visible optics, buried and fence-line sensors, and analytics tuned to reduce false alarms from wildlife and weather.

Household and vendor vetting

Background screening for staff, contractors and recurring vendors, with access windows and credentials that expire when the job does.

Architectural integration

We work with architects at design stage to place safe rooms, conduit, gate geometry and sightlines without compromising the design intent.

Remote monitoring

The Global Operations Center watches alarms and camera analytics in real time and dispatches the on-site team or local responders.

Seasonal homes

Vacant-period protection with scheduled patrols, environmental sensors and pre-arrival sweeps before the family returns.

Protocol

How an engagement unfolds.

Every step is documented, every handover named, and every operation closes with a written debrief.

  1. Survey

    Site and household assessment

    Walk the property at day and night, interview the household and map how people actually move.

  2. Design

    Layered security design

    Detect, assess, delay and respond, drawn as layers from the outer boundary to the safe room.

  3. Integrate

    Build with your architects

    Specifications delivered to your design team, installers vetted and work supervised.

  4. Staff

    Resident team in place

    Estate agents selected for the household, briefed on routines and introduced gradually.

  5. Review

    Quarterly testing

    Red-team walk-ins, alarm drills and system health reports every quarter.
