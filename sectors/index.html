Sectors & principals

Who we protect

Every principal carries a different kind of visibility. Programs are shaped around the person, the family or the institution, never around a standard package.

Family offices & UHNW families

Protection for every generation, coordinated with the family office: school runs, residences, travel and the digital exposure that connects them.

  • Family protection officers
  • Estate and seasonal homes
  • Digital footprint hardening

Corporate executives & boards

Executive protection, board meeting security and roadshow travel that keeps leaders focused, with reporting suitable for audit and governance committees.

  • Executive protection
  • Boardroom TSCM sweeps
  • Roadshow and AGM security

Visiting dignitaries & delegations

Receiving teams for delegations and senior officials, coordinated with protocol offices and local authorities.

  • Delegation liaison
  • Motorcade planning
  • Venue and hotel advance

Art, luxury collections & auctions

Security for collections in residence and in motion: previews, auctions, loans and private viewings, planned with conservators and insurers.

  • Object movement plans

Public figures & talent

Protection for people whose recognition is the risk: appearances, tours, fixated-person management and homes that must stay private.

  • Appearance and tour security
  • Threat assessment
  • Residential privacy

Adaptation

How programs bend to the principal.

The same doctrine, expressed differently for every life it protects.

Visibility

We calibrate how seen the detail should be: invisible for a family weekend, measured for a board meeting, overt during a period of elevated threat.

Rhythm

Officers learn routines, preferences and the people who matter, so protection fits the day instead of interrupting it.

Governance

For corporate clients, reporting is built for audit and risk committees. For families, it is built for the family office and its advisors.

Continuity

Consistent officers across years, documented knowledge and planned handovers when people change.
