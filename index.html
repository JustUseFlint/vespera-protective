Our position

We design protection the way architects design light: felt in every room, noticed in none.

Vespera works for families, executives and institutions whose visibility brings risk. We plan every engagement as a single program, so the officer at the door, the analyst watching the open-source picture and the driver choosing a quieter street are all working from the same brief.

Our people come from protective services, specialist policing and intelligence work, and are selected for temperament first. They are trained to de-escalate, to move early and to treat discretion as part of the protection itself.
